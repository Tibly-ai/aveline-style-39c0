Our Studio

A quiet world where felt becomes memory.

Every creature that leaves our studio starts as a handful of raw wool and a single needle. We shape ears, noses, and tiny paws one poke at a time, working slowly until each character feels like it has its own small life. AvelineStyleCorner is a one-person studio, and every doll that reaches you has been held, shaped, and checked by the same two hands.

We make elves, foxes, mice, and other woodland folk meant to sit on a windowsill, a bookshelf, or a desk and quietly keep you company.
